THIRD-PARTY NOTICES — BillVyse
==============================

Almost everything you see in this application was made for it. The artwork in
particular is hand-written geometry in source code, not licensed imagery:

  dashboard/app/components/Illustration.js   empty-state scenes, per business type
  dashboard/app/components/CategoryArt.js    (storefront) product tile motifs
  frontend/app/supplier/StateArt.js          supplier portal state scenes
  frontend/app/components/Icons.js           the storefront's own icon set
  public/billvyse-*.png, brand-*.png         the BillVyse marks

No stock photography, no Freepik / Unsplash / Flaticon assets, and no
AI-generated raster images are used anywhere. That is deliberate: a stock
licence grants permission, not ownership, and an AI-generated raster has no
human author to register a copyright to in India. Drawn geometry is owned
outright, tints itself correctly in light and dark mode, and weighs a few
hundred bytes instead of ~80KB.
